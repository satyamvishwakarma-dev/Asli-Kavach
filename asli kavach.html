SYNTHETIC MEDIA VERIFICATION
MISSION

Truth shouldn't need a second guess.

Asli Kavach — "real shield" — is a verification layer for the audio and video you're not sure about. Drop in a file or check a live voice call, and get a clear read on whether it's genuine or AI-synthesized, with the signals that led to that call laid out in the open.

OUR MOTTO

If it's real, we say so. If it's synthetic, we show you exactly what gave it away — no black box, no guesswork.

Voice cloning and face-swap deepfakes are getting cheap and convincing fast — used for scam calls, fake news clips, and impersonation. Asli Kavach exists so that a genuine question — "is this real?" — has a fast, transparent, evidence-backed answer, not just a vibe check.
